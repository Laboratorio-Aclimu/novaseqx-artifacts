Each allele recurred as a sequencing artifact in at least 3 of 17 clinical exomes sequenced on the NovaSeq X: support on one strand only, mostly in the lowest quality bin, and present in only one of two overlapping mates. Most alleles are rare or absent from population databases; some are classified as pathogenic in ClinVar.

A listed allele is not automatically false: a patient may carry it. Before reporting a call at a listed position, check the reads: support on both strands, several reads of base quality ≥20 and agreement between overlapping mates point to a true variant; support on one strand, low base quality and disagreement between mates point to the artifact. The command-line check (nsx_artifact_check.py) does this from the BAM or CRAM and annotates the VCF. Confirm with an orthogonal method when in doubt.
